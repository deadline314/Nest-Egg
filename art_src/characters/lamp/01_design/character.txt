Lamp, a sixteen-year-old boy who works as the radio dispatcher of a harbour pawnshop, fully dressed, about six and a half heads tall, skinny, a little shorter than an adult. Messy short black hair, dark circles under alert eyes, a serious face trying to look older. He wears a too-big faded olive rain parka zipped to the chest with the hood down, the sleeves down to the wrists with one frayed cuff; a pair of old over-ear headphones around his neck, the cable mended with tape; a small handheld radio clipped to a chest strap with one small green lamp; baggy dark grey work trousers; muddy yellow rubber rain boots.
