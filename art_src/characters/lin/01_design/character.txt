Miss Lin, a sales agent for a housing company, a woman of thirty, clearly a woman: about seven heads tall, slim, standing very properly with a slight apologetic tilt of the head. A soft tired face with a small polite smile, dark circles hidden under light make-up, black hair in a neat low bun with a side-swept fringe and one strand come loose. She wears a short cream-white company uniform jacket that ends at the belt line, well above the thighs, slightly too big in the shoulders, with a small teal enamel pin on the lapel; a pale blue blouse buttoned to the neck; straight dark teal trousers with a pressed crease gone soft at the knees; low black court shoes worn down at the heel; a thin gold wristwatch.
