Old Banyan, a herbal tea seller of seventy-two who has kept the same market stall all his life, an old man: about six and a half heads tall, thin and wiry with knobbly elbows and big hands, standing upright with only a slight forward set of the neck. A long kind face with deep wrinkles, drooping eyelids, large ears, a wispy white chin beard, a nearly bald head with a fringe of white hair, brown sun-spotted skin. He wears a faded short-sleeved shirt printed with a small pale leaf pattern, open over a white ribbed undershirt; loose dark grey cotton trousers rolled above the ankles; brown plastic sandals; a thin checked towel around the neck; a small cloth money pouch tied at the belt.
