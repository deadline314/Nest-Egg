Ali, a woman of twenty-seven who works the night shift at a harbour clinic lounge and wants to open a noodle stall; clearly a young woman, about seven heads tall, slight build. Chin-length dark brown hair tucked behind the ear with one plain green clip, tired kind eyes with faint shadows under them, a small closed-mouth smile. She wears a washed-out pale teal short-sleeved work tunic with a pen in the breast pocket; a short dark waist apron that ends at mid-thigh, with an old stain and a folded dish towel tucked into its string; plain grey trousers worn thin at the knee; old cream canvas sneakers with grey laces.
