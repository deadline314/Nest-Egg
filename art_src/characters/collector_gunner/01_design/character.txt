A harbour debt collector gunman, a man in his thirties, about seven heads tall, lean and wiry. Slicked-back dark hair coming loose, stubble, tired narrow eyes, a slightly crooked nose, a toothpick in the corner of his mouth. He wears a faded wine-red vinyl jacket that ends at the belt, zipped half way, the collar cracked with age, the sleeves down to the wrists; a cheap cream shirt with the collar open; an empty brown leather shoulder-holster strap across the chest; black trousers gone shiny at the knees; a small ledger pouch on the belt; worn black leather shoes with scuffed toes.
