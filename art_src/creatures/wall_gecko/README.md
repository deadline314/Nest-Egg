## 提示詞（英文原文）

design：
> A mechanical-organic gecko creature clinging to a surface, seen from the side facing right: a low flat lean body pressed close to the surface, four splayed limbs each ending in wide round sticky toe pads, a wedge-shaped head with a wide mouth and two large glowing magenta-pink eyes, and a long thick tapering tail curling up gently at the back (left). Dark teal-green scaly skin with metal armor plates along the spine, pale underbelly, and glowing magenta-pink dots along the flanks and toe pads. Crawling pose with the front-right and back-left limbs reaching forward. Strict side view facing right, centered in the picture. Nothing else in the picture: no wall, no ground, no shadow, no text, no other objects.

## 取捨與注意

- 畫的是「在地面或牆面上爬」的側視姿勢（四肢在身體下方），不是真正貼在牆上的視角。要爬牆時由動畫器把整隻旋轉 90 度、倒掛時轉 180 度。
- 洋紅發光點在洋紅色預覽底色上看不見，在灰色或黑色背景上才清楚。
- 尾巴向上大幅捲曲（圓環狀），如要做尾巴彈簧骨，需把尾巴從身體切出來；原圖中尾根與身體相連。
- 每隻腳有 4 到 5 根帶趾墊的趾，圖中近側與遠側肢體重疊在一起，切部件時要自行分遠近側。
